THE PROBLEM

645 million Africans online.
Zero local protection.

"SIM swaps drain bank accounts overnight. BVN leaks enable identity theft in minutes. Phishing attacks target mobile money users daily. African internet users have no defense. No local tool exists for them."

No Local Solutions

Western tools are built for Western threats. They don't understand BVN leaks, mobile money fraud, or SIM swap attacks. African users are left to defend themselves with tools that weren't made for them — or nothing at all.

Data Breaches

When a local bank, a global platform, or a Nigerian fintech is breached, millions of African credentials are exposed. Most users never find out until accounts are drained and damage is done. Tirenify closes this gap in seconds.

SIM Swap Fraud

A criminal calls your network provider, convinces them they're you, and walks away with your number. Every account linked to that number is now theirs. It happens in minutes. No Western security product is built to detect or prevent it.

The Cost of Waiting

Every month without a solution is another month of stolen savings, compromised identities, and eroded digital trust. The problem doesn't pause while we build. It compounds. That's why we started before we had funding.

THE FOUNDER
Daniel Adeleye Founder, Tirenify · Akure, Nigeria

I'm Daniel Adeleye — 18 years old, student at FUTA, self-taught developer, building Africa's security company from Akure, Nigeria.

My father passed away when I was 14. Before that, our family was stable. After, it wasn't. I don't tell this story for sympathy. I tell it because it taught me that circumstances don't determine outcomes. You do.

I live here. I use the same slow internet, the same mobile money apps, the same platforms that get breached. I understand the threats African users face because I face them too. I'm not building this from across the Atlantic. I'm building it from the inside.

I started Tirenify with zero capital. I'm still building it with zero capital. 78 people have used it. It works. Capital doesn't create the mission — it accelerates it. I'm building regardless. The question is how fast.
