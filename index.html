THE CHALLENGE

The problem no one
is solving.

"Over 1 billion Africans are online. Zero affordable, locally-built digital security tools exist for them."

No Local Solutions

Existing tools are expensive, not localized, and built for Western threats. African realities — BVN leaks, mobile money fraud, SIM swaps — are ignored entirely.

Data Breaches

When LinkedIn, Ticketmaster, or a local bank is breached, millions of African credentials are exposed. Most users never find out until the damage is done.

SIM Swap Fraud

Criminals exploit weak carrier verification to hijack numbers and drain accounts. It's an African-specific attack vector. No Western product is built to detect it.

The Cost of Waiting

Every month without a solution is another month of stolen identities, lost savings, and eroded digital trust across the continent. This problem compounds.
